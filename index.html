  1. 🤝
    Step 1

    Kickoff and Project Brief

    I learn about your goals, establish the timeline and cost, gather your materials and preferences, adapt the prototype structure, and agree on the sections with you.

  2. 🎨
    Step 2

    Design, Layout, and Visuals

    I select the style, colors, and font pairings, establish a balanced grid and spacing, and refine the interface and usability for desktop and mobile.

  3. 📝

    I finalize headlines and offers to complement the design, naturally incorporate target keywords, and configure meta tags and basic SEO structure.

  4. 🛠️
    Step 4

    Analytics, Legal
    Compliance, and Testing

    I set up lead forms, analytics, a privacy policy, and required consent options. I optimize loading speed and test the website across devices.

  5. 🚀
    Step 5

    Launch and Handoff

    I deploy the website to its live domain, submit a test inquiry, and submit pages for indexing. You receive all access credentials and operating instructions.

FAQ

Answers to Your Most Common Questions

What determines the cost of a project?

I work across industries and with clients in different countries, so every quote is tailored to the project. Pricing depends on the market, business goals, number of pages, languages, and features. The cost, timeline, and scope of work are determined individually after discussing the project.

Can I see your portfolio?

Of course! I’ll send you a separate link to my portfolio upon request.

How long does development take?

Timelines depend on the website type and other factors. A landing page typically takes one day to two weeks, while a multi-page website or online store takes at least three weeks. We agree on the exact schedule and milestones before work begins.

Can I manage my website myself after launch, including editing text, prices, and photos?

Yes. After launch, you receive full access and ownership. Ongoing management depends on the approach we choose: for custom-coded websites, I can set up an easy-to-use admin panel for basic edits or handle support myself. With a website builder such as Tilda, you can easily edit everything in its visual editor.

What if I don’t have copy or photos ready?

That’s not a problem; most clients start this way. I’ll help identify your key messages, write conversion-focused copy, and recommend photos to take or source quality visuals myself.

What if I’m not happy with the result?

My process is structured in stages, so the final result won’t come as a surprise. We first approve the prototype and structure, then the design concept using the hero section, and only then do I design the remaining pages. We review and refine each step until you feel it’s right.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. I stay available after handoff. You can reach out for technical support, new pages, SEO articles, or ad campaign setup whenever you’re ready to scale.

I currently get clients through social media. Do I really need a website?

Social media is great for nurturing leads, but algorithms and hard-to-find information can cost you customers. A website is your own platform, original product, and digital asset that works 24/7, independent of social media algorithms and outages. It also lets you attract customers through search (Yandex and Google) and run paid search campaigns.
